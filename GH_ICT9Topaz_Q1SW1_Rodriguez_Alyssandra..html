ABBA

ABBA was a legendary Swedish pop group formed in Stockholm in 1972. The band consisted of Agnetha Fältskog, Björn Ulvaeus, Benny Andersson, and Anni-Frid "Frida" Lyngstad. Their name is an acronym made from the first letters of their first names. They became one of the most successful music bands in history.
